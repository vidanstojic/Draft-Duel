## Full description (≤ 4000 characters)

```
Draft Duel is a quick 1v1 auction game. You and your rival start with the same points. Cards go up for auction one at a time, and the higher total wins.

HOW IT PLAYS
• 10 cards, 20 points each. The opener bids first; raise by 1, 2 or 5, or pass and let your rival take the card at their price.
• Ratings stay hidden while you bid. You see a card's rating when you win it, and your rival's cards at the final reveal.
• Run out of points and your rival picks who gets the rest, so every point counts.
• Default rules: each player ends with exactly 5 cards. Don't want a card? As the opener you can hand it over to your rival for a small fee that rises each time.

THEMES
• Football: players from Europe's top five leagues.
• Movies: 60 films across six genres.
• Food: every match is one dish. Collect the ingredients that belong in it; the wrong ones cost you points.
• Basketball: sample pack.

MODES
• Play vs bot: six bots with different styles, from a gut-feeling fan to a scout who plans every bid. Works offline.
• Same device: two players on one phone, either a split screen with the phone lying flat between you, or passing the phone.
• Custom rules: change points, cards per match, card limit, hand-over price, turn time and whether ratings are visible.

No accounts, no ads, no data collected.
```
