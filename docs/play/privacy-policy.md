# Draft Duel — Privacy Policy

*Effective date: 1 November 2026*

Draft Duel ("the app") is developed by `<developer name>`. This policy explains what the app does
with information about you.

## Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for an account, does not collect personal information, does not
use analytics or advertising services, and does not request internet access. Matches against
bots and on the same device run entirely on your phone.

## Information stored on your device

While the app is open it keeps the match you are playing and your last chosen rules in memory.
Nothing is sent anywhere, and this is cleared when the app is closed.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from
anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a later version adds online play, crash reporting or analytics, this policy will be updated
before that version is released, and the date above will change.
